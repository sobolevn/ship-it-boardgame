# House rules

Here are various unofficial rule sets that people use when playing Ship IT! with their friends.


## @sobolevn

- "Manual" can attach social rituals to any mechanic in the game. For example, you have to quack every time you play an attack or defense card
- "Manual" can stay on the table as a reminder instead of sitting in the discard pile until the end of the game. It still counts as a discarded card
